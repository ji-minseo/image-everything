IMAGE EVERYTHING / PRIVACY

Your files, your choices.

LAST UPDATED October 31, 2026

This policy describes information handling when you use Image Everything. Processing behavior varies by tool, browser and any third-party services loaded by the page.

Local file processing

Supported image, PDF and video tools perform their core file transformations within your browser. When a tool indicates local processing, its selected file contents are not intentionally sent to an Image Everything application server to perform that transformation. A page may still request scripts, model files, fonts or other resources from third parties. Check the details on the tool page.

External scripts and AI model downloads

Some features, including AI background removal, need external JavaScript libraries or downloadable models. These providers may receive normal requests for those assets and process technical request information such as IP address. Such requests are distinct from uploading an image for processing. Browser/network behavior and model availability may vary.

Technical data

Hosting, security, analytics or delivery providers may process information such as IP address, browser version, device type, referring page, pages visited and request timestamps to maintain the site, prevent abuse and understand reliability.

Cookies and advertising

Image Everything may use browser storage, cookies and third-party services for functionality, analytics and advertisements. Pages that load Google advertising services may allow Google and other third-party vendors to use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads according to their policies and applicable consent choices.

Manage Google's personalized advertising choices at Google My Ad Center. Other advertising opt-out information is available at AboutAds.info. Learn about Google's data practices on partner sites at Google's partner-site information page. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily remove ads.

Consent and your controls

Where required by applicable law, consent choices should be provided before optional advertising or analytics technologies are used. The interface and available settings may depend on the services enabled and your location. You can also control cookies or site storage through your browser settings.

Important file privacy limits

Removing metadata does not guarantee anonymity or removal of every proprietary field, cryptographic provenance record or visible location clue. Before publishing sensitive images or documents, inspect both the output and any information visible in the content. Do not use online tools for sensitive documents unless you are comfortable with their capabilities and limitations.

Links, children and updates

Linked third-party sites follow their own privacy policies. Image Everything is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy may be updated as features or providers change; the latest published version will show its update date.

Contact

For privacy concerns or questions, email info.everytinytool@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
