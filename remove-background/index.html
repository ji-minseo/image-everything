How to remove the background from an image

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo and choose Remove background. The browser downloads a machine-learning model on first use and runs the cutout locally. Once processing is complete, you can compare the original to the transparent result and select a new background color.

For the best results, use an image where the subject stands out from the background. High contrast and clear edges help with portraits, ecommerce product pictures, stickers and presentation graphics. Complex hair, fine glass and overlapping objects may not be separated perfectly.

Transparent PNG or solid-color JPG?

Transparent PNG preserves the empty backdrop so you can place the subject into another design. JPG cannot preserve transparency, so selecting a white, black or custom color will combine the cutout with that solid background.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove image backgrounds for free?

Yes. This browser-based tool offers automatic background removal with no sign-up. A model is downloaded into the browser on first use, so a modern browser and a reliable internet connection are recommended.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Your image is read and processed locally by the model in your browser. The JavaScript library and model weights are downloaded from third-party hosting, but your photo isn't uploaded by this tool.

How do I save an image with a transparent background?

Keep Transparent selected and choose Download transparent PNG. A JPG cannot preserve transparent pixels, so PNG is the right format for use in designs or over other photos.

Can I change the background to white?

Yes. After removing the original background, select White or Black, or use Custom to pick a color. The solid-background version downloads as JPG.

Why does the AI take time to start?

The background removal model requires a first-time download of roughly 45 MB and local preparation. Browsers often cache the model, so later runs may start faster, but speed depends on your device and connection.

Which images work best?

A clear foreground subject with visible edges produces better results. Fine hair, furry objects, transparent objects, and busy backgrounds are more difficult, and manual cleanup may still be needed.

Does the tool work on mobile phones?

Modern phones may run the model, but AI inference can require substantial memory. Large photos, older devices or certain browsers may fail. The limit is 12 MB and 16 megapixels per image.
